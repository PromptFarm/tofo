# Support

- **Bug or unexpected behavior?** Open a [GitHub issue](../../issues) — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-bugs) for what to include.
- **Question about how something works?** Check [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) first, then open a [discussion](../../discussions) if it's not answered there.
- **Security issue?** Don't use a public issue — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

There's no dedicated support team behind this — it's maintained on a best-effort basis. Clear, reproducible reports get looked at fastest.
